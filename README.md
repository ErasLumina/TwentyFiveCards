# ============================================================
#  25 CARDS  (2 players)
#
#  SETUP — 2 full decks (104 cards), shuffled together:
#    * deal each player 5 cards  -> HAND
#    * deal each player 25 cards -> GOAL DECK (top card face-up)
#    * remaining 49 cards        -> shared DRAW pile
#    * each player has 4 PARKING slots (LIFO stacks)
#
#  THE MIDDLE — unlimited "packs". Each pack builds A -> 2 -> ... -> Q
#  (suits don't matter). A complete A-Q pack is shuffled back into
#  the DRAW pile.
#
#  YOUR TURN (must start with 5 cards in hand):
#    Play ONE card onto a pack. The card may come from:
#      - the FACE-UP TOP of your goal deck  (preferred — this is how
#        you win), or
#      - your HAND, or
#      - the TOP of one of your parking slots.
#    It must be the next rank a pack needs (or an Ace to start a new
#    pack). KING is the PUPPET (joker) and counts as ANY rank.
#    After playing, refill your hand to 5 from the draw pile.
#    If you cannot play anything, you MUST park one hand card
#    (any slot) and the turn passes.
#
#  WIN — first to empty their 25-card goal deck wins.
#  BLOCK — dump hand cards to complete packs (recycling them to the
#  draw pile) and to steal the ranks your opponent needs next.
# ============================================================
